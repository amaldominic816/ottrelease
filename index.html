Understanding Theatrical to OTT Windows

Indian cinema typically observes a 4 to 8-week window between theatrical distribution and digital premieres. Blockbuster hits that perform exceptionally well in theaters may extend their theatrical exclusivity, while smaller releases often transition to OTT platforms within 30 days.

Where to Stream Malayalam Cinema Online

Regional cinema has found massive global viewership on platforms like Disney+ Hotstar, Amazon Prime Video, and SonyLIV. Regional niche platforms such as ManoramaMAX and Saina Play also release exclusive direct-to-digital films and festival favorites.

How OTT Release Dates Are Verified

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some movies show "OTT Date: TBA"?

Streaming rights are sometimes negotiated after the theatrical run concludes. When a distributor has not finalized digital rights, the title remains marked as TBA until officially announced.

Can I watch full movies directly on this portal?

No. OTTRadar provides release schedules, official trailers, and direct routing to verified streaming services to ensure creators are supported legitimately.
